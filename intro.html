How a shift works

Read it. Build it. Serve it.

You're at the drive-thru window. Cars keep pulling up, and every customer describes a secret-menu item instead of naming it. Work out what they mean before they lose patience.

  1. A car pulls up

    🙂“Crunchy, but folded. Beefy, with something green.”

    The bar under the hint is their patience. It drains while you think.

  2. Decode the hint

    Every hint word points to one ingredient. Crunchy means taco shell, beefy means beef, green means lettuce. The decoder lists them all.

  3. Build and serve

    Tap 3 or 4 ingredients in any order, then hit Serve it. Tap one again to take it back.

  4. Beat the clock

    0:10

    The whole shift lasts 60 seconds. The clock ticks out loud for the last 10.

Scoring and rules

Fast and right wins

+100
Correct order

Every order you get right is worth 100 points.

+0–100
Speed bonus

The more patience the customer has left when you serve, the bigger the bonus.

×2
Combo

Get 3 orders right in a row and every order after that scores double, until you miss.

✗
Wrong order

The customer tells you the real recipe, loses 30% of their patience, and your combo resets. Try again.

💡 5s
Free clue

Waiting more than 5 seconds? The customer gives away one ingredient.

3 out
Walk-outs

A customer whose patience runs out drives off. Three walk-outs close the shop early.

Tips from the night shift

Get on the board

  • Pick the base first: crunchy is a taco shell, soft is a tortilla. Every order has exactly one.
  • Count the clues. Most hints name 3 ingredients; later in the shift, orders grow to 4.
  • A wrong guess isn't the end. The customer shows you the recipe, so rebuild it fast for the points.
  • Protect your combo. Three right in a row doubles everything, which is where the big scores come from.
About the game

Made for the Secret Recipe Game Jam

  • Theme: a secret recipe from the world of Pizza Hut, KFC or Taco Bell. Here the secret is the off-menu order itself.
  • Limitation card: 60-Second Run. A full run never lasts longer than a minute.
  • Built as one HTML file: the 3D scene uses Three.js, and the sound is generated live with Web Audio. No image or audio files, no build step.
  • Scores are saved in your browser, so the Top 5 board shows players on the same device.
